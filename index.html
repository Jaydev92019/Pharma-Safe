How ADR reporting works

A report is a starting point for review—not proof that a medicine caused a reaction.

Record symptoms and when they began.

02 / DOCUMENT

A qualified reviewer evaluates available information.

Improve awareness

Medicine safety essentials

Stay informed. Seek advice.

Use medicines as advised by a qualified healthcare professional and report suspected reactions through appropriate official channels.

Never ignore a suspected reaction

Contact a healthcare professional for advice. Do not change a prescribed medicine without appropriate medical guidance. Seek urgent care for severe symptoms.

What is an ADR?

An adverse drug reaction is a harmful and unintended response to a medicine when it is used at doses normally used in humans for prevention, diagnosis or treatment, or modification of physiological function.
